## Section 3 — Gallbladder, liver, and metabolic

### 3.1 Set realistic expectations for Urdafalk

Ursodeoxycholic acid dissolves **small (<5–10 mm), radiolucent, pure cholesterol stones in a functioning gallbladder**, and even then takes 6–24 months with a high recurrence rate. ## Section 5 — Nutrition

The framework is **MIND/DASH-Mediterranean adapted to Indonesian food**, with two modifications: strict sodium control, and gallbladder-aware fat distribution.

### 5.1 The gallbladder nuance

Counterintuitively, an **extremely low-fat diet is wrong** for someone with gallstones — it causes biliary stasis and can worsen stone formation. The correct approach is **moderate fat, distributed across regular meals**, avoiding large single fatty loads (which trigger strong gallbladder contraction and colic). No skipped meals, no prolonged fasting, no crash diets.

### 5.2 Practical Indonesian-context guidance

**Increase:**
- **Ikan** — especially *kembung* (Indian mackerel), *sardin*, *tongkol*. Cheap, locally available, high in omega-3. Target 3–4×/week, grilled/steamed (*bakar/kukus*), not fried.
- **Tempe and tahu** — excellent protein, daily. Steamed, grilled, or lightly sautéed rather than *goreng*.
- **Sayur** — target 5+ servings/day of vegetables and fruit. *Bayam, kangkung, brokoli, buncis, wortel.* Prepare with minimal salt and oil.
- **Buah** — berries where available; papaya, guava, oranges, apples otherwise.
- **Kacang-kacangan** — a small daily handful of unsalted nuts.
- **Whole grains** — *beras merah* or *beras coklat*, oats. Reduce white rice portion and pair with protein and vegetables.
- **Olive oil or canola** for finishing; limit total cooking oil.
- **Water** — adequate hydration, relevant to the renal calcification too.
- **Protein target: ~1.2 g/kg body weight/day**, distributed across meals. This is higher than typical intake and is essential for preserving muscle mass through her 60s and 70s.

**Reduce sharply (the sodium sources):**
- *Garam, kecap asin, kecap manis, saus tiram*, MSG-heavy seasoning packets, *Royco/Masako* stock cubes
- *Ikan asin, telur asin, terasi, petis*
- *Kerupuk*, instant noodles, packaged snacks, canned/processed foods
- Restaurant and *warung* food — the largest hidden sodium source

**Reduce (fat load and gallbladder triggers):**
- Heavy *santan* dishes: *rendang, gulai, opor, soto betawi* — occasional, small portions, not weekly staples
- *Gorengan* — fried snacks, *ayam goreng*, *pisang goreng*
- Fatty meats, offal, skin

**Eliminate or minimize:**
- Sugar-sweetened drinks (*teh manis, es campur*, sweetened condensed milk)
- Alcohol — relevant to both BP and GGT

**🆕 Coconut oil — no (asked 2026-08-05).** Virgin coconut oil is **82–90% saturated fat** — higher than butter or lard — and controlled trials consistently show it **raises LDL** versus unsaturated oils. For a secondary-prevention patient targeting LDL <1.8, using it as a daily added fat is counterproductive. (Occasional *santan* in cooking is a portion question — different issue.)

**Olive oil — yes, and it was the right instinct.** Drizzling **extra virgin olive oil** over vegetables — exactly what Mama proposed — is one of the highest-value swaps available: it is the core fat of the MIND/Mediterranean pattern and it improves absorption of the fat-soluble vitamins in her *sayur*.
- **Spray for roasting:** fine, but **read the label** — many local sprays are olive oil *blended* with soybean or palm oil. Look for 100% olive oil, or use a refillable pump mister filled with her own EVOO (cheaper and better).
- **Gallbladder note (§5.1):** moderate fat is *correct* for her — very-low-fat causes biliary stasis. Olive oil spread across regular meals is right; a large single fatty load is what triggers colic.

### 5.3 A realistic implementation approach

Do not attempt a total dietary overhaul. Sequence it:
1. **Weeks 1–2:** Remove stock cubes and *kecap asin* from cooking; substitute with *bawang putih, jahe, kunyit, serai, daun jeruk, cabai, ketumbar*. Indonesian cuisine has enormous flavor depth without salt.
2. **Weeks 3–4:** Increase fish to 3×/week; reduce fried foods to once per week.

4. **Ongoing:** Address restaurant/*warung* frequency.
