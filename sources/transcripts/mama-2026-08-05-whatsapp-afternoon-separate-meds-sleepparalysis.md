**Pattern across the night (hypnogram)**

- **REM appears in short bursts** (~23:00, ~00:45) with the **longest REM period around 04:00** — also typical, as REM bouts lengthen toward morning.
- About **six brief awakenings** (orange spikes) spread across the night, the longest around **02:30–03:00**; they total only 19 min.

**How it compares to typical adult sleep**
- **Total sleep (7 hr 46 min)** falls within the recommended 7–9 hours ✔️
- **Awake time (4%)** is normal — brief arousals are expected ✔️
- **Deep sleep (7%)** is a bit below the typical ~10–20%, and **REM (12%)** below the typical ~20–25%; the balance is made up by an unusually high proportion of **core sleep (77%)**.

*Data-note (so the numbers are not misread): the stage durations are as displayed by the device, and their sum (19 + 56 + 375 + 35 = 485 min) exceeds the stated time-in-bed (475 min) by ~10 min — a device rounding artifact, not a contradiction. The stage percentages are calculated against the stage sum, which is why they total 100% while time asleep (466 min) + awake (19 min) = 485 min.*

Overall: a solid, efficient night in duration and continuity, though the composition is skewed toward light sleep, with somewhat less deep and REM than average. (The "Ringer" bar at the top of the screenshot is the iPhone volume indicator, not part of the sleep data.)
